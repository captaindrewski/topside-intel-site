Topside Intel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Topside Intel ("the app") is an unofficial fan-made companion app for ARC Raiders, developed by Andrew Rodriguez. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

Data on your device

Your region choice, workbench and quest progress, Outpost progress, alert settings and a cached copy of the map schedule are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

Starts-soon alerts are local notifications scheduled by your device. No push token or other identifier is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

Network requests

To show current content, the app makes standard requests to these services:

  • arcraiders.com: public map-condition schedule
  • metaforge.app: fallback schedule data and item images
  • cdn.arctracker.io: item images
  • u.expo.dev (Expo): app content updates

Like any website, these servers may receive your IP address and basic device or request information as part of normal internet traffic, under their own privacy policies. The developer does not receive or have access to this information. Links you open from the app load in an in-app browser and are subject to each site's own policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
