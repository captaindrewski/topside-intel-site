Topside Intel Support

Topside Intel is an unofficial companion app for ARC Raiders players. It offers live map conditions by region, starts-soon alerts, an item database, workbench planning, quest guides and Outpost tracking.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or data corrections, email Captaindrewski1@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version and the screen where you saw the problem.

FAQ

Alerts don't arrive. Check Settings › Notifications › Topside Intel and make sure notifications are allowed. Alerts are scheduled on your device ahead of time, so open the app now and then to refresh the schedule.

The schedule looks out of date. When you are offline, the app shows the last cached schedule with its update time. Pull to refresh when you are back online.

Some item data is wrong after a game update. Game data comes from community sources and can lag behind patches. Corrections are welcome.

Do I need an account? No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
